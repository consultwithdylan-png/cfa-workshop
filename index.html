Questions

Before you sign up

Do I need a truck?

Yes — you'll need a truck or trailer to move couches. If you don't own one, you've got options: borrow from family, rent one by the hour from a home improvement store, partner with a friend who has one, or set aside part of your first profits for it. We'll cover all of these in the workshop.

I don't have money to start. Can I still do this?

The whole system is built around getting couches for free through store partnerships, so you're not buying inventory up front. Your main costs are cleaning supplies, gas, and truck access. We'll show you how to keep startup costs low and pay for them out of your first flips.

I've never sold anything before. Is that a problem?

No. Most of our students had no sales or furniture experience. You'll get the scripts and steps we use.

How much time does it take?

Most people start with a few evenings and a Saturday each week. You choose how many flips you take on.

Is the workshop really free?

Yes. You'll learn the full 3-part system. At the end I'll share how to join the Academy if you want hands-on help — totally optional.

Will there be a replay?

Show up live if you can — that's where the Q&A happens. Register either way and we'll let you know about a replay.
