What if I don't have a truck?

You just need a way to get couches around. A lot of our students have U-Haul add a hitch to their regular, everyday car, and then rent U-Haul trailers for very cheap.

What if I've never sold anything before?

Perfect. That means you won't bring bad habits. Plus, the beautiful thing about this business is you don't really need to sell at all. The products sell themselves!

Are people actually buying used couches right now?

Absolutely. In fact, demand is higher than ever because new furniture is overpriced and backordered. People love high-quality secondhand furniture that looks great — and I'll show you how to make each piece look like a showroom steal.
